## Response Architectures

### LQS — Label → Question → Silence
Name the emotion without judgement → Ask a calibrated question → Wait.
Use when: Prospect is emotional, vague, or defensive.

### MIC — Mirror → Implication Chain → Permission Close
Repeat their last 2-3 words as a question → Surface the cost of inaction → Give them an easy yes.
Use when: Stated objection feels like a smokescreen. Need to find the real concern.

### AAR — Accusation Audit → Reversal
Say what they're thinking before they can → Pause → Redirect with a calibrated question.
Use when: High-resistance prospect. Has heard every sales script before.

### FCA — Future Pace → Cost Anchor → Autonomy Return
Help them visualize the future with the problem unsolved → Make the current cost explicit → Let them choose.
Use when: Prospect is stuck in present state. Cannot see past current constraints.

## The Silence Guide

| Technique | Silence Duration | What You're Waiting For |
|-----------|-----------------|------------------------|
| After labeling | 2-3 seconds | Confirmation or correction |
| After calibrated question | 5-8 seconds | Real answer, not defensive reflex |
| After mirror | 2-4 seconds | Elaboration on the real concern |
| After "what would need to be true" | 8-10 seconds | Their criteria, fully formed |
| After accusation audit | 3-5 seconds | Defences to drop |
| After 1-10 scale question | 4-6 seconds | Their honest read, not social answer |
| After "just not interested" response | 5 seconds | Whether there's a real reason |
